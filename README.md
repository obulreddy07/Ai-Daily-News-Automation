As an Automation Developer, I wanted a system that keeps me updated without eating into my time — so I built one.
 
Here's how it works:
 ⏰ Scheduled Trigger — runs automatically, every morning
 📡 RSS Read — pulls fresh articles from multiple news sources
 🎯 Limit — narrows it down to the 7 most relevant stories
 🧩 Aggregate — structures them into a clean digest (News 1 → News 7, with headline + full article link)
 🤖 Google Gemini (LLM Chain) — analyzes the batch and picks the Top Trending story of the day
 📩 Auto-delivered to my inbox — a ready-to-read digest, zero manual effort
 
The result? A personal AI news analyst that curates, summarizes, and delivers — while I focus on building.
 
This is a small example of what automation + AI can do when combined right: turning a repetitive daily task into a fully autonomous system.
